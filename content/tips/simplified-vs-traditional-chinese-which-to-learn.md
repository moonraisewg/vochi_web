Simplified or Traditional? Everyone learning Chinese hits this question in week one. The answer is not "one is easier" — it is "which one matches your target use". Here is the honest breakdown.

## Where each is used

- **Simplified**: Mainland China, Singapore, Malaysia (mostly), most international textbooks
- **Traditional**: Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau, most overseas Chinatowns pre-1980s

## The actual difference

Simplified reduced ~2,000 characters, mostly by cutting strokes. Grammar identical. Spoken Mandarin identical (aside from Taiwan/HK accents).

Examples:

| Simplified | Traditional | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 学 | 學 | study |
| 国 | 國 | country |
| 车 | 車 | car |
| 门 | 門 | door |
| 龙 | 龍 | dragon |

## Which is easier?

Simplified is objectively easier to write — fewer strokes. But once you read fluently, both feel similar to a native. If you plan to only read (menus, subtitles), the difference shrinks fast.

## Decision matrix

| Goal | Learn |
| --- | --- |
| Work with mainland Chinese companies | Simplified |
| Live/study in Taiwan | Traditional |
| Move to Hong Kong | Traditional (and Cantonese) |
| HSK exam | Simplified (HSK uses it) |
| TOCFL exam | Traditional (Taiwan's version) |
| Read classical Chinese, poetry | Traditional |
| Understand overseas Chinese communities | Both, start with the one the community uses |

## Can I learn one, then read the other?

Yes. Most learners who master ~1,500 characters in one script can read the other with 2–3 months of dedicated cross-reading. The mappings are mostly regular.

## What if I am still undecided?

Default to Simplified. Reasons:

- HSK (the standard global exam) uses it
- 90% of textbooks, apps, and online content use it
- Mainland has 4× the population; you will hit it more often
- Switching to Traditional later is easier than the reverse

## The one thing you cannot fake

You have to pick one for your first 1,000 characters. Trying to learn both simultaneously doubles your load with no benefit. Pick, drill for 6 months, then decide about the second.
