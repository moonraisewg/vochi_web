HSK 3 adds 300 words on top of HSK 1 and HSK 2, bringing your cumulative vocabulary to 600. This is where Mandarin stops being memorized phrases and starts being real conversation — you can talk about how you feel, describe the weather, explain a minor illness, and survive a day at the office. Here's the list, grouped by theme, with example sentences you can actually reuse.

A quick note on standards: this list follows the older HSK 2.0 syllabus (six levels, 150 / 150 / 300 / 600 / 1,300 / 2,500 cumulative words), which is still what most textbooks, apps, and test centers use. In 2021, China's Center for Language Education and Cooperation released HSK 3.0, a nine-level standard with much larger targets — its Level 3 requires roughly 1,000 cumulative words, not 600. If your course or exam specifies HSK 3.0, confirm which word list your program actually tests before relying on this one.

## What HSK 3 covers

- Emotions and reactions (worried, afraid, sad, satisfied, caring about something)
- Weather, seasons, and nature words
- Body parts and minor illness (fever, cold, aches)
- Travel basics (airport, passport, station, elevator)
- Office and study vocabulary (meetings, colleagues, review, grades)
- Degree words and connectors that make sentences sound natural (越...越, 一直, 极了)

If you haven't locked down [HSK 1](/tips/hsk-1-starter-vocabulary-guide) or [HSK 2](/tips/hsk-2-vocabulary-list-with-examples) yet, start there — HSK 3 assumes you already know those 300 words and builds on them fast.

## Sample from each cluster

### Emotions and attitudes (45 words)

- 害怕 (hàipà, to be afraid): 我害怕黑。 *I'm afraid of the dark.*
- 担心 (dānxīn, to worry): 别担心,没问题。 *Don't worry, it's not a problem.*
- 着急 (zháojí, anxious): 你别着急,慢慢说。 *Don't be anxious, take your time.*
- 满意 (mǎnyì, satisfied): 我对这次考试很满意。 *I'm satisfied with this exam.*
- 相信 (xiāngxìn, to believe): 我相信你能做到。 *I believe you can do it.*

### Degree words and connectors (35 words)

- 简单 (jiǎndān, simple): 这个问题很简单。 *This question is simple.*
- 越 (yuè, the more): 汉语越学越有意思。 *The more you study Chinese, the more interesting it gets.*
- 一直 (yìzhí, always/continuously): 我一直想学汉语。 *I've always wanted to learn Chinese.*
- 极 (jí, extremely): 这个电影好看极了。 *This movie is extremely good.*
- 打算 (dǎsuàn, to plan): 我打算去中国旅游。 *I plan to travel to China.*

### Weather and nature (35 words)

- 天气 (tiānqì, weather): 今天天气怎么样? *How's the weather today?*
- 云 (yún, cloud): 天上有很多云。 *There are a lot of clouds in the sky.*
- 刮风 (guāfēng, to be windy): 外面刮风了。 *It's windy outside.*
- 树 (shù, tree): 门前有一棵树。 *There's a tree in front of the door.*
- 环境 (huánjìng, environment): 这里的环境很好。 *The environment here is very good.*

### Health and the body (45 words)

- 疼 (téng, to hurt): 我头疼。 *My head hurts.*
- 发烧 (fāshāo, to have a fever): 孩子发烧了。 *The child has a fever.*
- 感冒 (gǎnmào, to catch a cold): 我感冒了,要吃药。 *I've caught a cold, I need to take medicine.*
- 生病 (shēngbìng, to get sick): 我生病了,不能上班。 *I'm sick, I can't go to work.*
- 饿 (è, hungry): 我很饿,想吃饭。 *I'm hungry, I want to eat.*

### Travel and places (35 words)

- 护照 (hùzhào, passport): 出国要带护照。 *You need to bring your passport to go abroad.*
- 机场 (jīchǎng, airport): 我们在机场见面吧。 *Let's meet at the airport.*
- 电梯 (diàntī, elevator): 电梯坏了,我们走楼梯吧。 *The elevator is broken, let's take the stairs.*
- 站 (zhàn, station/stop): 下一站是哪儿? *What's the next stop?*
- 迟到 (chídào, to be late): 今天我上班迟到了。 *I was late for work today.*

### Work, study, and communication (55 words)

- 会议 (huìyì, meeting): 明天有一个会议。 *There's a meeting tomorrow.*
- 同事 (tóngshì, colleague): 他是我的同事。 *He is my colleague.*
- 复习 (fùxí, to review): 考试前要好好复习。 *You need to review well before the exam.*
- 成绩 (chéngjì, grades/results): 他的成绩很好。 *His grades are very good.*
- 忘记 (wàngjì, to forget): 我忘记他的名字了。 *I forgot his name.*
- 解决 (jiějué, to solve): 这个问题已经解决了。 *This problem has already been solved.*

## 45-day plan

- 7 new/day × 45 ≈ 300 words
- All new HSK 3 cards + all due HSK 1/HSK 2 reviews
- One example sentence per card, read aloud on review
- Miss a day and FSRS just reschedules — you don't lose the streak, only a little time

## Why example sentences matter more at HSK 3

HSK 1 and 2 are mostly nouns and simple verbs — easy to brute-force from a single-word translation. HSK 3 introduces words that are defined by how they're used, not by what they mean in isolation: 极 only makes sense as 好极了 (never 极好了), and 越...越 needs both halves of the sentence to click. A card that shows just "极 = extremely" teaches you a fact you can't use. A card with a full sentence, resurfaced right before you'd forget it, teaches you the pattern along with the word — which is also why the scheduling algorithm behind your reviews matters; see [FSRS vs SM-2: what changed](/tips/fsrs-vs-sm2-what-changed) if you want the short version of why most spaced-repetition apps moved on from the old SM-2 scheduler.
