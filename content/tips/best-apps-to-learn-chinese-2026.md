There is no single "best" app for Mandarin. Different jobs, different tools. Here is what each is actually good at, in 2026.

## Quick verdict

| App | Best at | Weak at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HelloChinese | Beginner grammar drills | Retention past HSK 2 |
| Duolingo | Habit-forming reminders | Chinese specifically — thin content |
| Pleco | Dictionary and OCR | Not a study tool |
| Anki + FSRS | Long-term retention | Cold-start UX |
| Du Chinese | Graded reading | Vocabulary review |
| Vô chi | FSRS + always-visible pet reminder | Grammar lessons (not the focus) |

## HelloChinese

Structured lessons for HSK 1–3, good tone drills, native audio. Free tier is enough to reach HSK 2. Past HSK 3, its own reviews thin out — you need SRS elsewhere.

## Duolingo (Chinese)

Good for building a daily habit if you have none. Course is shorter and less rigorous than HelloChinese's. Not recommended as the only source.

## Pleco

Not a course. The dictionary. Free, works offline, best OCR for signs and menus. Everyone learning Chinese ends up owning it.

## Anki (with FSRS enabled)

The gold standard for retention. Community decks cover every HSK level. Ugly UI, steep first hour. Turn on FSRS in Settings (default in 23.10+) — cuts reviews ~40%.

## Du Chinese

Graded reading with audio and tap-to-translate. Excellent for post-HSK 2, especially before moving to native content.

## Vô chi

Desktop app for macOS and Windows. FSRS by default. What separates it from Anki: a small pet on your desktop that reminds you visually — no push notifications, no streak guilt. HSK 1–3 included; HSK 4–6 unlocks with a one-time 50,000₫ add-on. Offline, no telemetry.

## The realistic stack

- **Learn** grammar: HelloChinese
- **Look up** any word: Pleco
- **Retain** vocabulary: Anki or Vô chi (both FSRS)
- **Read** past HSK 2: Du Chinese

You do not need all five. Pick two — one for grammar, one for retention. Change up only when you plateau.
