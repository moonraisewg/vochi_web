Part 2 dài 2 phút. Không có ai cứu bạn. Nhưng đề Part 2 xoay quanh 8 chủ đề cố định. Có bộ từ đủ cho 8 chủ đề = có phao cho hầu hết mọi cue card.

## 8 chủ đề, 8 mini deck

Học 20 collocations + 5 idioms mỗi chủ đề. Tổng 200 collocations + 40 idioms. Đủ cho band 7.

## 1. Person (a person you admire)

- collocations: *look up to, role model, stand out from the crowd, down-to-earth, larger than life*
- idiom: *she wears many hats*, *he has a heart of gold*

## 2. Place (a place you enjoy visiting)

- collocations: *off the beaten path, tucked away, hustle and bustle, breathtaking scenery, hidden gem*
- idiom: *a stone's throw away*, *middle of nowhere*

## 3. Object (an object important to you)

- collocations: *cherished possession, hand-me-down, sentimental value, well-worn, family heirloom*
- idiom: *worth its weight in gold*

## 4. Event (a memorable event)

- collocations: *milestone moment, lifelong memory, exceed expectations, in full swing, hit it off*
- idiom: *once in a blue moon*, *the highlight of the year*

## 5. Experience (a time when you...)

- collocations: *life-changing, eye-opening, out of my comfort zone, learn the hard way, a wake-up call*
- idiom: *bite off more than you can chew*

## 6. Activity (a hobby)

- collocations: *hooked on, get the hang of, master a skill, unwind after work, second nature*
- idiom: *my cup of tea*, *the icing on the cake*

## 7. Media (a book/film/podcast)

- collocations: *page-turner, binge-worthy, thought-provoking, gripping storyline, well-crafted*
- idiom: *hits close to home*, *food for thought*

## 8. Decision (a difficult decision)

- collocations: *weigh the pros and cons, at a crossroads, sleep on it, no turning back, in hindsight*
- idiom: *between a rock and a hard place*

## Cách nhớ 240 cụm này

Đưa vào SRS. Front: cụm/idiom. Back: nghĩa + câu ví dụ Part 2 dài 20 từ. FSRS xếp lịch — 6 tuần retention ~90%.
