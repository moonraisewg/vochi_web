Band 6.0 lên 7.0 không cần từ khó hơn. Cần collocation đúng chỗ. Cambridge scoring rubric gọi đây là "less common lexical items" — tức là cụm cố định band 7 khác band 6.

## Vì sao collocation quan trọng hơn từ vựng đơn

Band 6: *"Environment is polluted a lot by cars."*
Band 7: *"Vehicle emissions have contributed significantly to air pollution."*

Từ dùng giống nhau. Collocation khác. Điểm khác.

## 5 chủ đề, 50 collocations

### Environment (10)

*carbon footprint, greenhouse gas emissions, mitigate climate change, adopt sustainable practices, deplete natural resources, phase out fossil fuels, biodiversity loss, environmental degradation, curb pollution, renewable energy sources*

### Technology (10)

*technological advancement, digital literacy, bridge the digital divide, automate manual tasks, foster innovation, data privacy concerns, cutting-edge technology, disrupt traditional industries, streamline workflows, artificial intelligence adoption*

### Education (10)

*promote critical thinking, foster lifelong learning, equip students with skills, address educational inequality, tailored curriculum, hands-on experience, rote memorization, standardized testing, academic performance, vocational training*

### Health (10)

*combat obesity, tackle mental health issues, sedentary lifestyle, raise public awareness, preventive healthcare, universal healthcare access, chronic diseases, mental well-being, junk food consumption, physical inactivity*

### Society (10)

*bridge generational gaps, promote social cohesion, tackle inequality, foster inclusivity, cultural preservation, urban migration, ageing population, work-life balance, community engagement, social integration*

## Cách dùng: 1 collocation/đoạn thân

Rải 5 collocations trong 2 đoạn thân bài (250 từ). Đừng dồn một chỗ — examiner đánh dấu "mechanical". Rải mỏng = tự nhiên.

## Cách nhớ: SRS + câu ví dụ

Front: collocation. Back: nghĩa + 1 câu Task 2 dài 25 từ. FSRS xếp lịch — 4 tuần retention 90%.

## Common mistake

Đừng ép collocation không hợp chủ đề. "Phase out fossil fuels" trong essay Education = kỳ. Học đúng cluster mới ra đúng chỗ.
