HSK 1 is 150 words. Sounds small, but they cover greetings, family, food, time, transport, and the top 20 verbs in daily Mandarin. Learn them well and you can survive a trip to Shanghai or Taipei.

## What HSK 1 actually contains

- **Pronouns**: 我 (I), 你 (you), 他 (he), 她 (she), 我们 (we)
- **Everyday verbs**: 是, 有, 想, 吃, 喝, 看, 听, 说, 去, 来, 做
- **Numbers and time**: 一–十, 百, 千, 现在, 今天, 明天, 年, 月, 星期
- **Family and people**: 妈妈, 爸爸, 老师, 学生, 朋友, 先生, 小姐
- **Question words**: 什么, 谁, 哪, 哪儿, 几, 多少, 怎么

## 30-day plan (10 min/day)

Split 150 words into 6 chunks of 25. Learn 5 new/day, review all due. Rest days on 5 and 6 of each week.

| Week | Focus | New words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pronouns, numbers 1–10 | 25 |
| 2 | Time, days, months | 25 |
| 3 | Family, people | 25 |
| 4 | Food, drink, verbs | 25 |
| 5 | Places, transport | 25 |
| 6 | Question words, review | 25 |

## Character vs pinyin

Learn both from day 1. Reading pinyin only is a trap — you plateau at HSK 2 and cannot read menus or signs. Start with the character on the front, pinyin + meaning on the back.

## Tones matter more than you think

妈 (mā, mom) vs 马 (mǎ, horse) is not a joke. Say each word aloud with the tone. Vô chi shows the tone mark next to every card.

## Why SRS wins for characters

Character shape decays fast without review. FSRS schedules review right before you would forget, so you keep 90% retention on 3× fewer reps than paper flashcards.
