Every English speaker who quits Mandarin quits for the same reason: characters. Grammar is easier than Spanish, tones are learnable in a month, but 3,000 characters for HSK 6 looks impossible. It is not. Here is the method that works.

## 1. Learn radicals first, not characters

Chinese has ~214 traditional radicals, but 90% of common characters use just ~50. Learn those 50 as their own deck. Every new character will then feel like assembling LEGO instead of copying random strokes.

Example: 好 (good) = 女 (woman) + 子 (child). Once you know both radicals, 好 sticks in under 30 seconds.

## 2. Card front: character. Card back: pinyin + tone + meaning + one sentence

Skipping the sentence is the single most common SRS mistake. A sentence stores the character in three encodings — visual, phonetic, and contextual — and retention doubles.

## 3. Use FSRS, not SM-2

Anki's classic algorithm (SM-2) treats every card the same. FSRS (default in Anki 23.10+ and in Vô chi) models each card as difficulty, stability, and retrievability. Result: ~40% fewer reviews for the same 90% retention. On a 3,000-character HSK 6 deck, that saves hundreds of hours.

## 4. Write once per day. No more.

Motor memory helps. Writing every character every day is overkill. Write only the new ones. Reviews stay digital.

## 5. Split reading and production decks

Recognition (see character → know meaning) is 4× faster to build than production (see meaning → recall character). Front-load recognition, add production only for high-frequency characters (top 500).

## 6. Use a desktop pet as your review reminder

Notifications are ignored. A small creature living on your desktop is not — you glance at it and remember. Vô chi's pet naps until a card is due, then wakes up. No streak guilt, no push notifications.

## Sample daily loop (10 min)

1. Review all due cards (~5 min)
2. Learn 5 new characters, decompose each into radicals (~4 min)
3. Write the 5 new ones once by hand (~1 min)

At 5 new/day, HSK 3 (600 chars) takes 4 months. HSK 6 (2,500 chars new) takes 18 months. Steady beats crammed.
