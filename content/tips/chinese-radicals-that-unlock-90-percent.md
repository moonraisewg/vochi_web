There are 214 traditional radicals in Chinese. You do not need them all. 50 of them make up ~90% of characters you meet at HSK 1–4. Learn those 50 first — each new character then becomes recognizable, not random.

## Why radicals matter

Characters are not drawn stroke by stroke. They are assembled from components. 好 (good) is 女 (woman) + 子 (child). 妈 (mom) is 女 (woman) + 马 (horse, phonetic). Once you know 女, both stick faster.

## The 50 highest-frequency radicals

### People and body (12)

- 人/亻 (rén, person): 你, 他, 们
- 女 (nǚ, woman): 好, 妈, 姐
- 子 (zǐ, child): 好, 学, 字
- 口 (kǒu, mouth): 吃, 喝, 叫
- 目 (mù, eye): 看, 睡, 眼
- 耳 (ěr, ear): 听, 聋
- 手/扌 (shǒu, hand): 打, 拿, 提
- 心/忄 (xīn, heart): 想, 忙, 快
- 足 (zú, foot): 跑, 跳
- 力 (lì, strength): 男, 助
- 首 (shǒu, head): 道
- 齿 (chǐ, tooth): 齡

### Nature (10)

- 日 (rì, sun/day): 明, 时, 早
- 月 (yuè, moon/month): 明, 期
- 水/氵 (shuǐ, water): 河, 湖, 洗
- 火/灬 (huǒ, fire): 热, 烧
- 木 (mù, wood): 林, 树, 校
- 山 (shān, mountain): 岛
- 石 (shí, stone): 破
- 土 (tǔ, earth): 地, 场
- 田 (tián, field): 男, 界
- 雨 (yǔ, rain): 雪, 电

### Objects (10)

- 门 (mén, door): 问, 间
- 车 (chē, vehicle): 轮
- 刀/刂 (dāo, knife): 分, 别
- 金/钅 (jīn, metal): 钱, 银
- 衣/衤 (yī, clothes): 衬, 补
- 食/饣 (shí, food): 饭, 饺
- 书 (shū, book): —
- 家 (jiā, family/home): —
- 王 (wáng, king/jade): 玩
- 皿 (mǐn, dish): 盘

### Motion and place (8)

- 走 (zǒu, walk): 起, 越
- 辶 (chuò, walk): 这, 那, 过
- 门 (see above)
- 广 (guǎng, shelter): 店, 床
- 宀 (mián, roof): 家, 安, 字
- 阝 (yì, city/hill): 都, 那
- 亠 (tóu, lid): 高, 京
- 冫 (bīng, ice): 冷, 冰

### Abstract markers (10)

- 一 (yī, one)
- 二 (èr, two)
- 十 (shí, ten)
- 大 (dà, big): 太
- 小 (xiǎo, small): 少
- 中 (zhōng, middle)
- 上 (shàng, up)
- 下 (xià, down)
- 又 (yòu, again/hand): 友, 双
- 也 (yě, also)

## How to drill them

- Put 50 radicals on 50 cards, own deck
- Front: radical. Back: meaning + 2 example characters
- 5 new/day × 10 days = deck done
- Keep reviewing for 3 weeks — after that, they surface automatically when meeting new characters

## What changes after 50

New characters take 30 seconds, not 5 minutes. HSK 4–6 grinds go from impossible to doable.
