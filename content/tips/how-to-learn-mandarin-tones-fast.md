Tones are not the hardest part of Chinese. Fixing them after you already sound wrong is. Do the 30 days below in month one, and you will never sound like a tourist.

## The 4 tones (and the neutral)

- **1st (ā)** — flat, high
- **2nd (á)** — rising, like a question
- **3rd (ǎ)** — dip and rise
- **4th (à)** — sharp fall
- **Neutral** — soft, unstressed

## Why most people fail

They practice single syllables (mā, má, mǎ, mà) then move on. Real Mandarin comes in pairs. 3-3 changes to 2-3. 4-4 shortens. Never trained → forever wrong.

## Week 1 — Isolated tones

- 5 minutes/day, one sound (mā, má, mǎ, mà, ma), record + replay
- Compare to a native audio clip
- Do not move on until a partner (or a language exchange) can pick out all four

## Week 2 — Minimal pairs

Drill these 20 pairs daily until they never confuse you:

| Pair | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 妈 mā / 麻 má | mom / hemp |
| 买 mǎi / 卖 mài | buy / sell |
| 是 shì / 十 shí | to be / ten |
| 问 wèn / 文 wén | ask / language |
| 睡 shuì / 水 shuǐ | sleep / water |

## Week 3 — Tone sandhi

Two rules cover 80% of cases:

1. **3-3 → 2-3**. 你好 is *ní hǎo*, not *nǐ hǎo*.
2. **不 (bù) → bú before 4th tone**. 不是 is *bú shì*, not *bù shì*.

Drill 10 examples/day for each.

## Week 4 — Shadowing

Play a 30-second native clip. Speak simultaneously, match rhythm + tone. Record. Compare. Repeat.

## Where SRS fits

Put each minimal pair on a card. FSRS schedules them right when you would confuse them again. Vô chi shows tone marks on every HSK card, so you cannot cheat by reading pinyin without pitch.
